# Status: ACTIVE FREEZE

## CORE HYPOTHESIS
"Structured progression materially improves inventor clarity and decision quality."
This is a hypothesis. Not yet proven.

## IN SCOPE
- IdeaState: maturity 0-2 only
- Domain: electronics/electrical inference only
- Gaps: PHYSICAL_FEASIBILITY, BOUNDARY_AMBIGUITY, MECHANISM_COMPLETENESS
- Transitions: deterministic PASS/WARN/BLOCK
- Memory: iteration delta (direction only)
- Questions: one per iteration, reframed after 3 stalls

## OUT OF SCOPE — FROZEN
- Medical / legal / automotive / regulatory engines
- FDA, CE, ISO, Alice doctrine
- Prior art search
- Multi-domain orchestration
- Uncertainty model (UNVERIFIABLE, HYPOTHETICAL)
- Maturity levels 3, 4, 5
- Scoring of any kind
- External document generation
- Expanding DECISION_PROGRESSION_MODEL.md before validation

## SUCCESS CRITERION (singular)
After 3-5 iterations: does the inventor articulate
their idea more precisely than when they started?

## FAILURE SIGNALS
1. Inventor games questions to pass without idea improving
2. Same gap open 5+ iterations without reframe
3. Questions unrelated to open gaps
4. Maturity changes without evidence change
5. Multiple questions per iteration
6. All gaps ACCEPTED_RISK with none CLOSED

## REVISION PROTOCOL
Freeze expands only after:
- 3 real ideas tested through LEVEL 0-2
- At least 1 genuine BLOCK understood by inventor
- At least 1 idea reached LEVEL 2 with documented clarity improvement
- Missing gap/rule identified through real usage, not anticipation

## FINAL WARNING
Platform risks becoming:
  - dynamic questionnaire
  - score optimization system
  - giant expert-system simulator
The freeze protects the progression hypothesis from premature complexity.
