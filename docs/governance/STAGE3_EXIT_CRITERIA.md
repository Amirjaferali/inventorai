## GOVERNING QUESTION

This document answers one question only:

What must be true before Stage 3 can legitimately be considered complete?

It does not answer: who authorizes progression, when progression occurs, how progression occurs, or what engine behavior follows. Those belong to Progression Logic and Transition Authorization -- neither of which is authorized by this document.

---

## 1. THREE LEVELS -- NEVER COLLAPSED

Three distinct levels govern Stage 3 completion. They must never be collapsed into one another.

**Level 1 -- Per-Gap Completion:**
Each of the three canonical gap types has been addressed to the satisfaction of its resolution conditions.

**Level 2 -- Stage-Level Completion:**
The three per-gap completions are coherent as an integrated whole. The inventor has demonstrated the integrated Stage 3 epistemic state, not merely three independent gap resolutions.

**Level 3 -- Progression Authorization:**
This level is NOT defined in this document. Progression authorization belongs to the Transition Authorization Mechanism (deferred per STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION §5.1). Stage-level completion (Level 2) is necessary but not sufficient for progression authorization.

---

## 2. ACTIVE RISK REGISTER

**Risk AS -- Exit Criteria / Progression Logic Boundary Collapse:**
This document defines what must be true. It does not define who authorizes, when, how, or what engine behavior follows. Any statement in this document that begins with 'the system shall' or 'progression occurs when' is a prohibited intrusion from Progression Logic.

**Risk AT -- Checklist Completion Reappearance:**
PMF resolved plus AI resolved plus EGA resolved does not equal Stage complete. The reason is established in STAGE3_GAP_RESOLUTION_MODEL §6.1: stage-level resolution requires cross-gap coherence (SL-R2) and exit characterization synthesis (SL-R3), neither of which can be derived from per-gap aggregation. This document preserves that distinction explicitly.

**Risk AU -- Evidence Availability Mismatch:**
Every exit condition in this document is classified as: Verifiable Today, Partially Verifiable Today, or Requires Future Infrastructure. This classification is explicit and honest.

**Risk AV -- False Closure:**
Formal compliance is not genuine readiness. The protections are: SL-R2 requires auditable cross-gap coherence (not pattern completion), SL-R3 requires synthesis across all three gap types (not individual demonstrations), and the Transfer of Reasoning test (SR-001 §3.7) distinguishes genuine development from protocol familiarity.

**Risk AW -- Exit Criteria Inflation:**
Exit criteria define evidence of capability, not evidence of perfection. A capable inventor who demonstrates genuine implementation-oriented reasoning should be able to satisfy these criteria. An inventor who cannot is not ready, regardless of effort. The distinction is preserved by grounding every criterion in resolution conditions already established -- not by adding new thresholds.

## 3. PER-GAP COMPLETION: PROBLEM_MECHANISM_FIT

### 3.1 Completion Definition

The PROBLEM_MECHANISM_FIT gap is complete when the inventor has demonstrated sufficient PMF-CAP that the relationship between their mechanism and their intended problem is no longer an unexamined assumption.

### 3.2 Completion Conditions

All three resolution conditions from STAGE3_GAP_RESOLUTION_MODEL must be satisfied:

PMF-EC1 (from PMF-R1): The inventor's problem articulation is stable and independent. It holds when the mechanism description is set aside. It does not collapse back into mechanism description under probing.

PMF-EC2 (from PMF-R2): The inventor's fit reasoning is causal and inventor-owned. The justification connects a specific mechanism property to a specific problem requirement. It is specific to the inventor's mechanism, not to the technology category.

PMF-EC3 (from PMF-R3): The inventor has acknowledged at least one condition under which the fit is limited or does not hold. The fit limit is specific to the inventor's mechanism-problem relationship, not a generic technology limitation.

### 3.3 Evidence Availability Classification

PMF-EC1: Verifiable Today. Stability under probing is observable within a single session using PMF-Q1 and PMF-Q1-P1.
PMF-EC2: Verifiable Today. Causal specificity is observable within a single session using PMF-Q2 and PMF-Q2-P1.
PMF-EC3: Verifiable Today. Fit boundary awareness is observable within a single session using PMF-Q3 and PMF-Q3-P1.

**PMF gap completion is fully verifiable today.**

### 3.4 False Closure Protection

An inventor who produces correct-sounding problem articulation that mirrors their mechanism description has not satisfied PMF-EC1. An inventor who asserts fit without a causal chain has not satisfied PMF-EC2. An inventor who cannot name any fit boundary has not satisfied PMF-EC3. Formal language is not sufficient -- the conditions require specific content.

## 4. PER-GAP COMPLETION: ASSUMPTION_INVENTORY

### 4.1 Completion Definition

The ASSUMPTION_INVENTORY gap is complete when the inventor has demonstrated sufficient AI-CAP that their load-bearing assumptions are no longer invisible -- named, classified, and at least partially discovered.

### 4.2 Completion Conditions

AI-EC1 (from AI-R1): The inventor has named at least one assumption that was not already identified as a Stage 2 gap. The assumption is specific to the inventor's mechanism-problem relationship established in PMF-CAP.

AI-EC2 (from AI-R2): The inventor has classified their assumptions by criticality -- distinguishing load-bearing from peripheral -- with reasoning that connects each assumption to a consequence for their specific implementation path. The classification is stable under probing.

AI-EC3 (from AI-R3): The inventor has identified at least one assumption they were not aware of making before Stage 3. This assumption is absent from Stage 2 gap records and from prior Stage 3 responses.

### 4.3 Evidence Availability Classification

AI-EC1: Verifiable Today. Observable within a single session using AI-Q1.
AI-EC2: Verifiable Today. Observable within a single session using AI-Q2 and AI-Q2-P1.
AI-EC3: Partially Verifiable Today. The discovery itself is observable within a session. Whether the assumption was truly unrecognized before requires comparison with Stage 2 records -- which requires session history. If Stage 2 records are unavailable, AI-EC3 is assessed through the inventor's testimony and plausibility of the discovery within the session context.

**AI gap completion is substantially verifiable today with a partial dependency on Stage 2 record availability for AI-EC3.**

### 4.4 False Closure Protection

An inventor who lists Stage 2 gaps relabeled as assumptions has not satisfied AI-EC1. An inventor who classifies all assumptions as load-bearing without justification has not satisfied AI-EC2. An inventor who claims no new assumptions were discovered is either complete (AI-EC3 may be satisfied by genuine exhaustion) or has not engaged with the question -- distinguished by the plausibility of their earlier assumption inventory depth.

## 5. PER-GAP COMPLETION: EXPERTISE_GAP_AWARENESS

### 5.1 Completion Definition

The EXPERTISE_GAP_AWARENESS gap is complete when the inventor has demonstrated sufficient EGA-CAP that the expertise required for their implementation path is no longer unacknowledged.

### 5.2 Completion Conditions

EGA-EC1 (from EGA-R1): The inventor has named expertise domains with justification for why their specific implementation requires them. The connection between expertise domain and implementation requirement is explicit and mechanism-specific.

EGA-EC2 (from EGA-R2): The inventor has assessed their own expertise level relative to the identified domains, distinguishing what they know from what they do not, with reasoning specific to their mechanism and background. The self-assessment is not generic.

EGA-EC3 (from EGA-R3): The inventor has articulated what would specifically happen to their implementation if the identified expertise gaps were not resolved. The consequence is mechanism-specific, not a generic statement about the importance of expertise.

### 5.3 Evidence Availability Classification

EGA-EC1: Verifiable Today. Observable within a single session using EGA-Q1 and EGA-Q1-P1.
EGA-EC2: Verifiable Today. Observable within a single session using EGA-Q2 and EGA-Q2-P1.
EGA-EC3: Verifiable Today. Observable within a single session using EGA-Q3 and EGA-Q3-P1.

**EGA gap completion is fully verifiable today.**

### 5.4 False Closure Protection

An inventor who lists expertise domains without connecting them to implementation requirements has not satisfied EGA-EC1. An inventor who claims expertise in all identified domains without reasoning has not satisfied EGA-EC2. An inventor who produces generic consequence statements not specific to their mechanism has not satisfied EGA-EC3.

## 6. STAGE-LEVEL COMPLETION

### 6.1 Why Stage-Level Completion Is Not the Sum of Per-Gap Completions

Risk AT identifies the failure mode: PMF complete plus AI complete plus EGA complete does not equal Stage 3 complete.

The reason is established in STAGE3_GAP_RESOLUTION_MODEL §6.1 and preserved here: an inventor who satisfies per-gap completion conditions independently but produces contradictions between them has not demonstrated the integrated Stage 3 epistemic state. Stage-level completion requires coherence and synthesis -- neither is derivable from per-gap aggregation.

### 6.2 Stage-Level Completion Conditions

**SL-EC1 (from SL-R1): All nine per-gap completion conditions are satisfied.**
PMF-EC1, PMF-EC2, PMF-EC3, AI-EC1, AI-EC2, AI-EC3, EGA-EC1, EGA-EC2, EGA-EC3 are all met.
Evidence availability: verifiable today with partial dependency on Stage 2 records for AI-EC3.

**SL-EC2 (from SL-R2): Cross-gap coherence is demonstrated.**
The three Auditable Contradiction Tests pass:
CCT-1 -- The problem articulation from PMF is consistent with the assumptions from AI. No contradiction between what the inventor says the mechanism is for and what they say they are assuming.
CCT-2 -- The load-bearing assumptions from AI are connected to the expertise gaps from EGA. Every load-bearing assumption has either a corresponding expertise gap or demonstrated inventor knowledge sufficient to not require external expertise.
CCT-3 -- The next action proposed by the inventor follows logically from PMF plus AI plus EGA combined. The action addresses the most critical uncertainty given the complete Stage 3 picture.
Evidence availability: verifiable today within a single session using COH-Q1, COH-Q2, COH-Q3.

**SL-EC3 (from SL-R3): The SA-001A §6 exit characterization is reached.**
The inventor demonstrates all four elements:
Element 1 -- Prioritized next action with justification tracing to PMF-CAP, AI-CAP, and EGA-CAP combined.
Element 2 -- Remaining unknowns named with precision, traceable to unresolved assumptions or unresolved expertise gaps.
Element 3 -- Proof-of-concept objective defined, connecting to a specific load-bearing assumption the inventor cannot currently validate.
Element 4 -- Uncertainty reduction articulated, comparing current state with Stage 2 exit state -- naming what was uncertain before and what is now better understood.
Evidence availability: Elements 1-3 are verifiable today within a single session. Element 4 requires comparison with Stage 2 exit state -- partially verifiable today if Stage 2 session records exist, otherwise assessed through inventor testimony.

| Condition | Verifiable Today | Partially Verifiable | Requires Future Infrastructure |
|---|---|---|---|
| SL-EC1 (nine per-gap conditions) | Mostly | AI-EC3 (Stage 2 record dependency) | Cross-session comparison |
| SL-EC2 (cross-gap coherence) | Yes -- CCT-1/2/3 within session | -- | -- |
| SL-EC3 Element 1 | Yes | -- | -- |
| SL-EC3 Element 2 | Yes | -- | -- |
| SL-EC3 Element 3 | Yes | -- | -- |

**Current honest assessment:** Stage 3 completion is substantially verifiable today. Full verification of AI-EC3 and SL-EC3 Element 4 depends on Stage 2 session record availability -- a persistence infrastructure gap documented in SR-001 §4.5 and GOVERNANCE-ROADMAP Priority 3.

### 6.4 False Closure Protection at Stage Level

An inventor who satisfies all nine per-gap conditions but produces contradictions between them has not satisfied SL-EC2. The CCTs are auditable -- contradiction either exists or does not.

An inventor who proposes a next action disconnected from their load-bearing assumptions has not satisfied SL-EC3 Element 1. The justification must trace to the combined Stage 3 picture, not to a pre-existing plan.

An inventor who names remaining unknowns that are unrelated to their assumption inventory has not satisfied SL-EC3 Element 2.

Protocol familiarity does not produce false closure because SL-EC2 and SL-EC3 require synthesis -- a cognitive act that cannot be performed by pattern completion.

## 7. WHAT STAGE-LEVEL COMPLETION DOES NOT DETERMINE

Stage-level completion (SL-EC1 through SL-EC3 satisfied) establishes that the inventor has demonstrated the Stage 3 epistemic state.

It does NOT establish:
- That progression to Stage 4 is authorized
- That the platform has verified completion
- That any engine state changes
- When the transition occurs
- Who authorizes the transition

These belong to the Transition Authorization Mechanism, which is deferred per STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION §5.1 and recorded as a mandatory precondition in STAGE3_DESIGN_AUTHORIZATION §4.1.

Stage-level completion is necessary for progression authorization. It is not sufficient.

---

## 8. CAPABILITY VS PERFECTION

Risk AW identifies the inflation risk: exit criteria that demand perfection rather than capability.

Every exit condition in this document is grounded in resolution conditions already established in STAGE3_GAP_RESOLUTION_MODEL. No new thresholds are introduced. The question for each condition is: has the inventor demonstrated this capability? Not: has the inventor demonstrated this capability flawlessly?

A capable inventor who reasons genuinely about their mechanism-problem relationship, names real assumptions they were not previously aware of, and identifies expertise gaps with consequence reasoning will satisfy these criteria. An inventor who produces technically correct but disconnected responses, recycled Stage 2 content, or generic statements that do not trace to their specific mechanism will not.

The distinction is capability evidence versus perfection performance. These criteria test the first.
