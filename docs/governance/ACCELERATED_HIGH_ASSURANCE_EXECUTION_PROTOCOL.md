## 1. Purpose and central principles

This document owns delivery mechanics; Lean §2 owns milestone authority and Lean §§3–5B
own proportionate evidence and review. A successful included action advances to the next
included action when its material safeguards are satisfied. This document supplies no
additional routine Owner approval stage.

**Standing loop-compression objective:** `FEWER LOOPS + SAME OR HIGHER ASSURANCE`.
Use differential reconstruction (§6), claim-scoped assurance (§7), consolidated correction
(§12), review convergence (§14) and a proportionate review interface (§15). Do not create a
second compression lifecycle.

**Anti-bureaucracy principle:** a control must prevent a material failure, supply necessary
proof or remove redundant work. Retire controls that do none of these under §26. Use an
existing PR description or one return message for material evidence; no acknowledgement
pages, compliance-only candidates or reports about unchanged reports.

**Evidence principle:** material verifiable claims have reproducible derivation. Machine
output proves facts, not authority or acceptance. Live Git/GitHub prove transient identity;
the Owner instruction and existing current authority surfaces establish execution scope.

## 3. Applicability

Apply these mechanics to authorized implementation, review, publication and merge in
proportion to the actual work. Read-only analysis has no candidate-delivery ceremony.
Lean §2 and the active mandate determine included actions and activation conditions; Lean
§§3–5B determine their evidence. Historical contracts and candidate records are evidence of
prior decisions, not current commands merely because they remain readable.

## 4. Execution modes (descriptive only)

Use only the distinction useful to the work: implementation/semantic change, bounded repair,
or mechanical identity verification. These descriptions are not another risk axis or a
mandatory lifecycle declaration. They do not prescribe separate START, bundle, review,
Owner acceptance, publication or merge stages. Apply Lean's risk model to each affected
behavior, then perform the actions actually included by the active mandate. A mixed change
receives the evidence needed for its highest affected risk without expanding unrelated scope.

## 5. Live-base inspection and reconciliation

Verify repository identity and inspect the live authoritative branch before construction,
publication and merge, and again when material concurrent movement is observed. Compare it
to the known base; a moved tip alone is not a STOP condition.

Inspect intervening commits for changes to scope, assumptions, authority, dependencies and
reviewed behavior. Reconcile safely within the mandate, using normal merge/fix commits or a
safe rebase before a new review when appropriate. A new head invalidates applicable old-head
checks and approvals; refresh affected evidence before delivery. Preserve reviewed objects
and verdicts (§9). Never force-push or destroy accepted/rejected evidence to reconcile a base.

Hold only affected work when movement creates a material conflict; resolve routine technical
conflicts autonomously. Escalate a reserved decision or invalidated mandate under Lean §10.
Global holds are limited to unverified identity, invalid whole-milestone authority, an unsafe
working state or a cross-cutting defect invalidating all remaining work.

## 6. Differential reconstruction

Default scope is the last verified authoritative truth plus the current delta, newly fired
obligations and relevant new Owner decisions. Reconstruct unchanged history only for a
material authority/continuity conflict, applicable full-audit trigger, high-risk strategic
reconsideration or explicit Owner request. Follow the single `CLAUDE.md` boot sequence.

Do not treat memory, a rejected candidate, or a historical snapshot as current authority.
A direct current Owner instruction has the effect and successor-durability requirements
specified by Lean §2; repository lag alone does not cancel an instruction actually received.

## 7. Assurance reuse (claim-scoped, dependency-scoped, self-invalidating)

Evidence inheritance attaches to a claim, not wholesale candidate acceptance. Where reuse
matters, identify the verified claim and source identity, relevant dependencies, what changed,
invalidation conditions and whether the claim is inherited, revalidated or invalidated.
Record this in the existing evidence account; no separate manifest is mandatory.

New material evidence can invalidate prior assurance. Previously accepted work is not immune
from contradictory evidence, but a new preference is not an invalidation. Independent review
and reuse follow Lean §5/§5B; a reviewer preserves their own judgment and independence.

## 8. Execution-Reachability Check

“Docs-only” is not proof of zero runtime effect. Before reusing a runtime conclusion, establish
that the changed artifacts are not execution/build/config/registry/loader reachable, or that
the dependencies supporting that conclusion remain unchanged. State the relevant basis.

Authority-changing prose may be high risk without needing unrelated application tests.
Workflow/configuration changes receive applicable structural and behavior validation. This
check determines relevant evidence; it does not create a new Owner approval or broad test run.

## 9. Reviewed identity and evidence preservation

Record a meaningful review subject by exact head, base/parents, tree and changed paths;
provide diffstat where useful or specifically required. “Frozen” means an immutable reviewed
commit object, not a ban on advancing a PR through ordinary fix commits.

A revised head is a new review subject. Keep earlier reviewed objects and their verdicts,
and refresh evidence affected by the revision. Do not amend, erase or relabel accepted or
rejected objects. A safe pre-review rebase is permitted under §5; once a head has been reviewed,
prefer append-only fix/merge commits, or preserve it before any explicitly permitted replacement.
No force-push or history deletion is implied. Use a fresh branch only for §12's material reasons.

Never require a candidate's own SHA in its tree. Git/GitHub and the review/return record own
transient identity; self-referential identity recording cannot require another candidate.

## 9A. Lead preparation and implementation (bounded)

Before material work, establish objective, current base, included authority, scope, acceptance
criteria, relevant owners, required evidence, material dependencies and reserved decisions.
Reuse the current mandate and PR description; do not create another preflight artifact or
repeat unchanged fields at every transition. Preparation is bounded by §6 and Lean risk.

Identify external inputs that cannot be supplied honestly by the execution Lead or AI review,
including participant, adviser or provider evidence. State which affected work they block,
what would resolve them and the latest safe gate. Continue unrelated authorized work. Do not
invent a reviewer identity, qualification, signature or external opinion.

The Owner-authorized Lead may implement directly or coordinate bounded sub-agents. Lead and
Creator functions may be performed by the same person/session for ordinary execution; actual
execution evidence is still required. Formal non-authoring independence under Lean §5 is
separate. A same-session child agent is assistance, not the formal independent reviewer.
The Owner is not routine message transport. Material delegated instructions still satisfy
§9A.1; direct implementation does not require writing and relaying an instruction to oneself.

OSP's canonical §3.2 already permits a later explicit Owner-authorized role change. The current
mandate selects the sole mutating Lead; no competing Lead, authority claim or replacement OSP
is created. Unknowns produce a scoped hold only when they materially prevent the affected
operation; routine missing technical information is investigated within scope.

## 10. Implementation and consolidated self-review

Complete authorized implementation and relevant checks, perform one dependency-bounded
consequence sweep and adversarial self-review under Lean §5A, correct material defects, and
present the meaningful candidate with its evidence. The historical “Creator Grill” name refers
to this same self-review; it is not an additional interview, report or lifecycle.

Sweep only direct/transitive dependencies affected by the change: callers, contracts,
cross-references, ownership statements and evidence claims. Do not expand into speculative
cleanup or untouched process owners. A material consequence outside the mandate holds that
work for the appropriate Owner decision; unrelated authorized work continues.

Required evidence must match the final review subject. Repeat only the affected review/checks
after a relevant change, failure or unresolved concern. A material unresolved defect blocks the
affected delivery; a non-blocking wording preference does not create a repair lifecycle.

## 11. Evidence integrity

Material counts, identities and execution results must be accurate and reproducible. Derive
available mechanical facts with tools; distinguish source-agent verification from current-agent
independent verification. Correct erroneous claims visibly against their subject and explain
material consequences. Never silently rewrite a historical verdict or fabricate a run.

## 12. Rejection handling and ordinary repair

Preserve each reviewed SHA and its verdict. A rejected SHA remains rejected evidence; it may
be an ancestor of a corrected PR head without inheriting acceptance or tainting a demonstrated
repair. Ordinary fix commits on the same PR are permitted. Review and tests for the corrected
head are proportionate to the changed behavior and invalidated dependencies under Lean §5B.

A fresh replacement branch/candidate is required only when preserving the old line would
materially contaminate evidence, the base or authority changed materially, safe repair is not
possible, or an explicit high-risk review decision requires replacement. State that reason;
never require a fresh same-base sibling merely because correction was necessary.

Default repair scope is previously proven claims plus authorized repair delta (§7).
Accumulate the reasonably discoverable material findings, perform one bounded correction
batch, then review closure of those findings. Do not intentionally split findings into
micro-rounds or re-prove unchanged facts without an invalidation trigger. Newly discovered
material evidence is not suppressed because a previous sweep missed it.

**Closed-finding finality:** reopening a closed/resolved/accepted finding requires new
repository evidence or changed higher authority, identification of the prior closure, the
material contradiction and why it matters now. A new stylistic or implementation preference
is insufficient. Consolidate repairs before requesting further review wherever practical.

## 13. Finding disposition

Use the existing classes, without creating another register:

- **BLOCKER:** safe/valid completion or required authority is absent; holds the affected operation.
- **MAJOR:** a material contradiction, scope/authority breach, substantive regression risk or
  implementation-invalid contract; holds affected delivery until resolved.
- **MINOR:** blocks only while material behavior, security, scope, truthful claims, identity
  or required evidence remains unresolved; optional tightening/style does not block.
- **OBSERVATION:** does not block the current operation.

Do not downgrade a material defect or inflate style into a gate. Resolve technical
classification using evidence; escalate only a reserved decision or material authority
conflict under Lean §10. Do not impose a whole-milestone hold for a local uncertainty.

A material deferred concern needs evidence that the current work can proceed, an existing
owner and return trigger/latest safe gate, discoverability where required by the Deferred
Obligations Register, and no unacceptable orphan or future risk. Pure wording preferences
need no invented deferred obligation. Non-blocking findings do not seed standalone repair
lifecycles; address them at the next legitimately authorized relevant touch when useful.

## 14. Review loop breaker

Request another round only for an explicit unresolved question that can materially change
acceptance. If no such question exists, proceed. Repeated issues converge to acceptance,
bounded repair or a reserved Owner decision; no arbitrary round limit suppresses new
material defects.

A new material invalidation of subject identity, scope, authority, dependencies or assurance
justifies fresh review of the affected subject and dependencies. Otherwise, when evidence is
sufficient, required review complete, material blockers zero and scope valid, advance to the
next included action. Reviewer-on-reviewer recursion is not a standing requirement.

If repeated same-family corrections falsify their common premise, assess whether the issue
is a bounded defect, wrong owner/contract boundary, missing dependency or architectural
limitation. Continue at the lowest viable level or present the material Owner decision with
options and a recommendation. This reasoning does not authorize new architecture and needs
no new register, protocol or review program.

## 15. Independent review interface

Lean §5/§5B exclusively determines when review is required, formal independence, test breadth
and evidence reuse. Neither a “formal closure” label nor document format creates an extra
review stage independently of its material semantic risk.

Provide the reviewer the exact review subject, authorized scope, delta, applicable acceptance
criteria, changed dependencies and available evidence. Prefer an accessible remote PR/head;
use exact-object local transfer under §17 when remote publication is unavailable or not yet
authorized. The reviewer verifies identity and examines actual evidence, not author assurance.

The same non-authoring reviewer may inspect a correction without re-onboarding to unchanged
history. Preserve the original verdict at its SHA, distinguish reused verified facts from
new conclusions, and refresh the evidence invalidated by the changed head. A verdict alone
is not proof of the underlying behavior. Begin review when a stable subject and sufficient
evidence permit meaningful scrutiny; do not manufacture an earlier empty review gate.

Formal closure of authority, canonical ownership, disputed obligations, release blockers or
acceptance criteria is classified for its actual consequence under Lean §§3–5B. Applicable
substantive return gates remain in force; closure wording alone cannot bypass them.

## 16. Reproducible delivery evidence

Keep one coherent evidence account in the existing PR or return message. It identifies the
head/base and changed scope, commands/environment, outcomes including material skips,
applicable reviewer verdict, unresolved material findings and any reserved decision.
Additional machine manifests are optional when useful for transfer or reproduction; do not
require separate evidence about evidence already available on the platform.

Git proves identity, topology, paths and merge state. Tests prove the behaviors they exercise.
Neither a script's exit code nor a reviewer label establishes authority, product meaning,
semantic ownership or a legitimate deferral. Those judgments need reasoned evidence.

## 17. Exact-object transfer (bundle when needed)

An accessible remote PR/head normally supplies review identity. A SHA-preserving bundle is
appropriate when a reviewer cannot access it, publication is not authorized, or an actual
transfer/offline requirement exists. A bundle is transport, not a universal lifecycle stage.

When used, record its prerequisite and head, verify the bundle/hash and demonstrate that
isolated fetch-back reproduces the exact intended commit, parents and tree. Retain rejected
objects/verdicts where needed without placing transient self-identities in the candidate tree.
Reviewer verification is proportional under Lean §5/§5B and the actual transfer mechanism.

## 18. Acceptance and reviewed-head identity

The active mandate under Lean §2 determines who accepts delivery and which actions are
included. Do not add personal Owner exact-SHA acceptance for an included routine action.
Required independent review does not itself grant authority; conversely, authorized work
still needs the evidence required by risk.

Acceptance attaches to the reviewed subject and satisfied criteria, not any later head.
A revision requires refreshed affected evidence under §§9/12/15. It does not by itself require
a new Owner decision. An explicit reserved decision, material expansion or stop condition
returns to the Owner; acceptance of one scope never closes another obligation implicitly.

## 19. Publication and PR verification

Check publication/PR authority in the active mandate. When those actions are included, push
and create/update the PR without another approval stage. If they are excluded, stop before
publication; a passed review cannot supply the missing authority. The bootstrap transition's
explicit preparation-only boundary remains effective until its authorized subsequent decisions.

Publish the intended commit objects through normal non-force push. Before merge, verify live
platform truth: authoritative target, exact head, current base, changed paths/content, truthful
PR description, required checks/review and mergeability. An ordinary repair advances the PR
head and invalidates affected prior evidence; it is not unauthorized merely for being a new SHA.

Detect material unexpected platform/content changes, including wrong target/head, unrelated
commits, altered authority claims, enabled deployment or an unauthorized merge mode. Harmless
rendering/metadata normalization is not a blocker. Routine PR title/body/review-request changes
inside the mandate need no separate approval. Never silently accept substantive mutation.

## 20. Deliberate merge and post-merge verification

Merge only when the active mandate expressly includes it and all activation conditions and
applicable safeguards have been verified. No additional Owner merge decision is created for
an included action. A missing mandate or reserved decision is not replaced by passing CI.

**Autonomous merge prerequisites:** CI passes on the actual candidate; required checks are
tied to the latest head; the authoritative branch is protected; force-push/deletion are
disabled; failed or missing checks block merge; the independent-review route required by risk
is usable; required review has accepted this subject; and a mechanical expected-head guard
prevents integrating an unreviewed revision. If any prerequisite is unavailable, hold merge
only. Do not assert autonomous merge safety from workflow text or permission flags alone.

Use **CREATE A MERGE COMMIT**. The Lead deliberately invokes merge after checking the exact
reviewed head, applicable reviews and required results. Use the platform's expected-head
argument (for example `--match-head-commit <EXACT_REVIEWED_HEAD>`); an immediate manual head
read alone is insufficient to prevent a race. Do not use auto-merge queues unless separately
shown safe and compatible within explicit authority. Do not bypass failed/missing checks,
commit directly to the authoritative branch, force-push or modify `main`.

Verify post-merge live Git/GitHub truth: PR is merged; merge parents match the integration
base and reviewed head; the integrated tree/diff matches the verified expected result; and
the merge is present in authoritative history. For a current-base tree-identical merge, prove
merge tree equals candidate tree and candidate-to-merge diff is empty. If the base changed,
apply §5 and refresh affected evidence for the expected integration result before merging;
do not claim tree equality when the integrated base makes it false. Concurrent later commits
do not undo a verified merge; inspect them rather than require the branch tip to stay frozen.

Record material resulting truth with its implementation where practical, under Lean §11.
A SHA, PR, parent, merge or lifecycle-label change alone requires no standalone governance
synchronization or mandatory YES/NO synchronization report. Verify identity automatically and
continue to the next included task; never start a status-only candidate about this delivery.

## 21. One concise delivery account

Evidence belongs in one existing PR/return account (§16), updated when material facts change.
Do not require separate task-start, candidate-return, review-compliance and Lead-compliance
blocks. An actual material deviation and its consequence must still be visible.

### 21.1 Preparation

Establish the bounded parameters in §9A and reuse them. No “SOP READ” certification or
separate execution-mode declaration is necessary.

### 21.2 Candidate return

State exact subject, scope, validation, applicable review, material findings and next included
action or genuine Owner decision. Do not hard-code Owner acceptance/merge requirements.
Do not create another artifact solely to repeat the same information.

### 21.3 Independent review evidence

Where review is required, the reviewer independently assesses the actual subject and material
evidence. Creator/Lead reasoning is not a substitute for execution results or independent
findings. A reviewer records one verdict with material findings and evidence limits.

### 21.4 Lead delivery decision

The Lead checks semantic correctness, applicable tests/review, authority and integration
safeguards before proceeding. Return to the Owner only under Lean's reserved-decision or
stop rules, not merely to confirm that an included routine action passed.
