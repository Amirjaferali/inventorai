## 1. Technology-first guidance

Any future D13 user-facing output must lead with the technical diagnosis. The
required output priority is:

- **A.** the exact unresolved technical subproblem;
- **B.** the missing technical information, evidence, measurements, documents,
  operating conditions, or constraints;
- **C.** the precise technology, technical topic, or subdomain to investigate;
- **D.** suitable technical search terms or bounded research topics;
- **E.** the required measurements, tests, simulations, tools, datasheet
  categories, standards categories, or technical evidence;
- **F.** what InventorAI can verify;
- **G.** what InventorAI cannot verify;
- **H.** the uncertainty, limitation, abstention, or stop condition;
- **I.** only when external expertise remains necessary, the general specialist
  category narrowly matched to the unresolved technical subproblem.

Items A through H must precede item I. The specialist category must never
replace the technical diagnosis.

---

## 2. Specialist category is subordinate and optional

A specialist category may be included only when:

- the technical diagnosis leaves a residual need for external expertise;
- the category is supported by evidence;
- the category is as technically narrow as the evidence permits.

InventorAI must avoid broad default guidance such as "Consult an electrical
engineer." Where evidence permits, it should identify a narrower category,
such as: embedded sensor-interface specialist; analog signal-conditioning
specialist; PCB signal-integrity specialist; PCB power-integrity specialist;
PCB thermal-design specialist; drone propulsion-system specialist;
flight-control integration specialist; battery and power-system specialist;
solar-system sizing specialist; energy-storage specialist; grid-integration
specialist.

These examples illustrate classification granularity only. They do not
authorize any listed domain. If no specialist category is evidence-supported,
InventorAI must abstain rather than invent or default to an overly broad
category.

---

## 3. No named-provider recommendation by default

InventorAI must not default to naming or recommending a specific individual, a
specific company, a specific institution, a specific consultancy, or a specific
commercial service provider.

The system identifies technical problems, technical topics, evidence needs,
verification methods, and specialist categories. It does not select vendors or
providers by default. Nothing in this decision authorizes commercial rankings,
referral arrangements, paid placement, or provider endorsement.

---

## 4. Internal expert governance remains separate

Preserve the distinction between:

- **A.** user-facing specialist-category guidance; and
- **B.** qualified technical experts and independent reviewers used internally
  to validate governed knowledge.

This owner decision changes the future user-facing output model. It does not
eliminate qualified technical validation, independent review, provenance
requirements, source-authority rules, abstention, contradiction handling, or
domain-package governance. Internal experts and reviewers are not user-facing
referral providers.

---

## 5. Future-domain boundary

Examples involving drones, PCB-specialized design, renewable energy, energy
storage, grid integration, or any other future field are illustrative only.
They authorize no product domain, research domain, knowledge package, taxonomy,
specialist map, implementation, or user-facing capability.

Each future domain requires a separately approved and validated domain package
and owner authorization. The current MVP scope remains
electronics/electrical-only.
