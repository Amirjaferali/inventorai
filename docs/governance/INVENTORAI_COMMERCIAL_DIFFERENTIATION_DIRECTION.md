## 2. Commercial Gap

InventorAI cannot justify subscription value merely through: more questions;
longer reports; generic recommendations; conversation storage; generic education;
telling the inventor what work to perform; or governance documents without
user-visible capability. None of these, alone or combined, is a defensible
commercial advantage over a general AI conversation.

## 3. Commercial Differentiation Thesis

The strongest defensible differentiation is that InventorAI preserves and evolves
a single traceable technical state that links: requirements; evidence;
assumptions; decisions; versions; implementation artifacts; tests; revisions; and
change impact.

A general AI may suggest or research. InventorAI must **govern, preserve, verify
truthfully, and evolve** the technical development state over time. This is a
direction, not a claim of present completeness: not all of these capabilities are
currently implemented, and this document authorizes none of them.

## 4. Primary Product Objective

Advancement of the idea is the primary product objective. Inventor learning may
occur but remains secondary; it must not become the platform's primary identity,
roadmap priority, or success metric.

Management question that governs every increment:

`What difficult work has the platform completed for the idea that the inventor
would otherwise have needed to perform manually or obtain from separate tools or
specialists?`

## 6. First-Increment Value Standard

Every proposed increment must identify:
1. the exact user task completed by the platform;
2. the difficult work no longer delegated to the inventor;
3. the artifact or decision produced;
4. the evidence and provenance preserved;
5. the user-visible competitive improvement demonstrated.

Documentation, governance, fields, questions, and labels alone do not satisfy
this standard.

## 7. Evidence and Truthfulness Direction

Preserved principles (the committed evidence/artifact and source-of-truth
contracts remain the controlling models; this section references, it does not
recreate or replace them):
- no invisible reliance on model memory as verified technical fact;
- facts, owner requirements, calculations, inferences, hypotheses, physical
  results, and specialist findings must remain distinguishable;
- unsupported values must not be promoted to compatibility, selection, safety, or
  readiness claims;
- a sourced candidate is not automatically compatible;
- generated output is not verified output;
- truthful blocking is a valid and valuable product outcome.

The controlling models are
`docs/governance/TECHNICAL_REALIZATION_EVIDENCE_AND_ARTIFACT_MODEL.md` and
`docs/governance/SUPPORTED_TECHNOLOGY_AND_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_CONTRACT.md`.

## 8. Decision and Change-Impact Direction

The first bounded change-impact behaviour must:
- retain stable direct-input identifiers;
- preserve a normalized direct-input snapshot;
- preserve the prior and current values;
- preserve the exact reason for change;
- identify the affected decision record and version;
- set the committed internal `validity_status=stale`;
- display `REVIEW REQUIRED` only as a user-facing label.

`REVIEW REQUIRED` is a user-facing label, not a committed internal status; the
committed internal status is `validity_status=stale`. This direction authorizes
no general dependency graph or transitive staleness engine in the first
increment.

## 10. Preserved Scope Boundaries

The current first-lane direction must not issue or implement: `technically_selected`;
`frozen`; final component selection; BOM; wiring; firmware; compilation;
simulation; tested or demonstrated claims; certification claims;
production-readiness claims; Path T; multi-domain expansion; or persistence
delivery.

`owner_preferred` records owner preference only. It does not establish technical validity, compatibility, safety, specialist approval, or a final technical selection.

`unresolved` is **not** a `decision_status`. An unresolved outcome must be
represented only through the committed readiness and blocking vocabulary
(`readiness_status` and `blocking_reasons[]`), without inventing a decision-status
value.
