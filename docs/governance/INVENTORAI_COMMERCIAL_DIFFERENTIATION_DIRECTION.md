## 2. Commercial Gap

InventorAI cannot justify subscription value merely through: more questions;
longer reports; generic recommendations; conversation storage; generic education;
telling the inventor what work to perform; or governance documents without
user-visible capability. None of these, alone or combined, is a defensible
commercial advantage over a general AI conversation.

## 3. Commercial Differentiation Thesis

The strongest defensible differentiation is that InventorAI preserves and evolves
a single traceable technical state that links: requirements; evidence;
assumptions; decisions; versions; implementation artifacts; tests; revisions; and
change impact.

A general AI may suggest or research. InventorAI must **govern, preserve, verify
truthfully, and evolve** the technical development state over time. This is a
direction, not a claim of present completeness: not all of these capabilities are
currently implemented, and this document authorizes none of them.

## 4. Primary Product Objective

Advancement of the idea is the primary product objective. Inventor learning may
occur but remains secondary; it must not become the platform's primary identity,
roadmap priority, or success metric.

Management question that governs every increment:

`What difficult work has the platform completed for the idea that the inventor
would otherwise have needed to perform manually or obtain from separate tools or
specialists?`

### 16.A — Owner-approved direction

InventorAI may eventually help a non-technical or technical inventor answer connected readiness
questions through **one integrated, evidence-derived assessment**, potentially encompassing:
technical readiness / TRL awareness · manufacturing readiness / MRL · integration readiness / IRL ·
whole-system (SRL-like) synthesis · risk assessment · opportunity validation · commercial and
adoption readiness · preliminary novelty / prior-art **evidence**.

**Every axis remains subject to its existing owner and to a later, separately authorized
implementation contract. None is implemented. None is authorized here. Listing an axis is not a
claim that it exists, works, or is scheduled.**

The intended user experience direction — recorded, not decided — is: derive every criterion that can
be derived from evidence already recorded; ask the user only when a missing fact is decision-relevant
and cannot be safely derived; reuse an answer across all affected assessments; and permit "I do not
know". The engine's existing dispositions (`answered` / `unknown` / `deferred` /
`provisional_assumption`, `engine/idea_state.py`) and the pure, non-mutating `derive_readiness()`
already embody this posture; nothing new is required to hold it as direction.

**Advisory output only where it is safely supported — truthful blocking is PRESERVED (binding).** An
advisory, provisional result may be returned ONLY where the recorded evidence safely supports one.
**Absence of decisive evidence must never be laundered into a low-confidence positive readiness
result.** Where evidence is missing, contradictory, or not decision-safe, the truthful outcome stands:
`UNKNOWN`, `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`, a withheld assessment, or a hold — each a **valid and
product-valuable result**, consistent with the standing Owner constraint that *"`UNKNOWN` /
`INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` / `HOLD` are valid outcomes and negative conclusions are product value"*. **It
is expressly NOT the direction that every incomplete state produces an assessment**, and "expose
missing evidence rather than block" is **not** recorded here as a rule.

**This record changes no decision policy.** It does **not** alter any existing pass /
pass-with-conditions / hold decision policy; does **not** weaken, lower or bypass any evidence
threshold; does **not** convert a withheld, negative or unknown outcome into a positive one; and does
**not** create any presumption toward producing a result. At the original recording, the exact outcome words were Owner policy vocabulary,
not implemented canonical tokens. §16.G now explicitly preserves the accepted future
vocabulary; neither record changes current state tokens, evidence thresholds or policy.

reviewable, testable prototype package—showing what is established, what is inferred,
what remains unknown, and what must be verified. The idea is primary; learning is
