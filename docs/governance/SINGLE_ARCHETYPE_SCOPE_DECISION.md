# SINGLE_ARCHETYPE_SCOPE_DECISION.md
## Owner Decision Record — Single User Archetype as MVP Scope

**Document ID:** SINGLE_ARCHETYPE_SCOPE_DECISION
**Governance Level:** Level 3
**Status:** OWNER DECISION RECORD
**Date:** 2026-06-04
**Depends on:** STRATEGIC_PRODUCT_VISION.md (L0)

## 1. THE DECISION

The single-user-archetype architecture of InventorAI was an MVP scope decision.
It was not a permanent product boundary decision.

Current archetype: Developing Inventor — possesses an invention concept,
lacks systematic domain expertise, benefits from structured reasoning development.

## 2. EVIDENCE BASIS

SPV §11 establishes the governing precedent:
"Commercial architecture is intentionally deferred to Stage 6.
This deferral is a sequencing decision, not an architectural exclusion."

The same logic applies to user archetypes. Deferral of additional archetypes
is a sequencing decision, not an architectural exclusion.

SPV §9 Deferred Scope pattern: deferred capabilities are sequencing decisions.
GOVERNANCE-ROADMAP "Expert Review Flow": deferred, not rejected.
INVENTORAI_PROJECT_STATE_FREEZE_v1.2: constraints framed as "MVP Scope."

No committed artifact explicitly excludes specialist or professional users.
Absence of multiple archetypes is a product of MVP scope, not prohibition.

## 3. WHAT THIS DECISION ESTABLISHES

Single archetype was MVP scope. Additional archetypes are within intended
product scope. No existing architecture is changed.

## 4. WHAT THIS DECISION DOES NOT ESTABLISH

Does not authorize Mode B or any other archetype design.
Does not authorize implementation changes.
Does not modify SA-001A, SR-001, or any governance document.
Does not authorize domain expansion or multi-domain architecture.

## 5. GOVERNANCE PROTECTION

The developing inventor archetype remains the only currently governed archetype.
All existing evaluation models and governance standards apply to this archetype only.
No future archetype may be designed without explicit owner authorization.
No future archetype may modify Stage 2 or Stage 3 rules.
No future archetype may weaken the Hierarchical Hybrid governing constraint.

*No implementation authorized by this document.*
*All existing governance constraints remain fully active.*
