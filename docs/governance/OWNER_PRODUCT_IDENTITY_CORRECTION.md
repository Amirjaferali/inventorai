## 3. Owner Correction

The product's primary identity is:

> A governed idea-development and cross-domain invention orchestration
> platform.

The primary subject of development is the idea.

Inventor or user learning may occur as a secondary effect, but it is
not the platform's primary product identity, principal success metric,
or governing roadmap objective.

Path N is intended for non-technical or non-specialist users who
provide an idea, problem, intended beneficiary, or relevant context.
The platform leads the idea-development journey, identifies relevant
domains, orchestrates cross-domain questioning and evidence, identifies
missing information, evidence, tools, expertise, tests, resources, and
unresolved dependencies, discloses system capability limitations,
preserves gaps and known unknowns, generates evidence-supported reports
or structured outputs, performs only supported simulation, synthesis,
assembly, or progression attempts, and clearly discloses any part it
cannot support. It must not falsely represent unsupported gaps as
solved.

Path T serves technical and engineering questioning contexts.

## 5. Corrected Product Identity

The following replaces the governing effect of §1 PLATFORM IDENTITY of
`docs/governance/STRATEGIC_PRODUCT_VISION.md`:

InventorAI is not an idea evaluator.
InventorAI is not an AI assistant.
InventorAI is not a product generator or business plan tool.
InventorAI is not a platform whose primary identity or governing
objective is inventor development or inventor growth.

**InventorAI is a governed idea-development and cross-domain invention
orchestration platform.**

The platform is a deterministic, domain-agnostic structured reasoning
engine. It leads an idea through a governed progression journey,
accumulating evidence, surfacing gaps and known unknowns, identifying
missing information and expertise, and producing honest structured
outputs. Inventor or user learning may occur as a beneficial secondary
effect of this process. That secondary effect has value and is not
denied; it is simply not what the platform is primarily built to
measure, optimize, or claim.

## 6. Primary Subject and Success Orientation

The following replaces the governing effect of §2 OWNER VISION LOCK,
§3 OWNER VISION STATEMENT, and §5A PLATFORM SUCCESS DEFINITION of
`docs/governance/STRATEGIC_PRODUCT_VISION.md`:

**Primary subject of development:** The idea.

**Primary governing objective:** Evidence-grounded progression of the
idea. This includes, where applicable and supported:

- Clearer problem and beneficiary definition
- Cross-domain decomposition of the idea
- Explicit preservation of unresolved gaps and known unknowns
- Identification of missing evidence, expertise, tools, tests, or
  resources
- Supported progression toward evaluation, simulation, assembly,
  implementation, or other justified next steps
- Transparent disclosure of unsupported or unresolved elements

This orientation does not claim that every idea must reach
implementation or commercial readiness. The platform's success is
measured by the honesty and completeness of what is established and
what is preserved as unknown — not by advancement to a particular
stage.

**Secondary effect:** Inventor or user reasoning capability may improve
as a consequence of guided idea development. This effect is real and
beneficial. It is not denied. However, it is not the platform's
primary identity, principal success metric, or governing roadmap
objective. No governance decision, feature, or evaluation criterion
may be made to optimize for inventor learning at the expense of honest
idea-state reporting.

**Continuity with prior governance principles:** The core governance
principles established in §7 of `STRATEGIC_PRODUCT_VISION.md` —
Principle 1 (inventor ownership of reasoning), Principle 2
(improvement not generation), and Principle 3 (multi-domain
integration vision) — remain fully binding and are not amended by this
document. The deterministic, replayable engine and the advisory-only
AI constraint established in §1 of `STRATEGIC_PRODUCT_VISION.md`
(other than the platform-identity sentence) remain in effect.

## 7. Path N

Path N serves non-technical or non-specialist users who provide an
idea, problem, intended beneficiary, or relevant context.

Under this amendment, the platform's role in Path N sessions is:

- Lead the idea-development journey
- Identify relevant domains
- Orchestrate cross-domain questioning and evidence accumulation
- Identify missing information, evidence, tools, expertise, tests,
  resources, and unresolved dependencies
- Disclose system capability limitations clearly and explicitly
- Preserve all gaps and known unknowns; do not silently close them
- Generate evidence-supported reports or structured outputs
- Perform only supported simulation, synthesis, assembly, or
  progression attempts
- Clearly disclose any part the platform cannot support

The platform must not falsely represent unsupported gaps as solved,
resolved, or closed.

Under this amendment, non-specialist questioning must translate
engineering parameters into accessible terms, defer questions the
non-specialist cannot yet answer, or record the gap as an unknown.
It must never demand engineering parameters the user cannot supply.
