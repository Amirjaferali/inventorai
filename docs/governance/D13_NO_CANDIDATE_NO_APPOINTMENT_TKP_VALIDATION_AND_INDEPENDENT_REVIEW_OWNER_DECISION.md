## 6. Separation of Review and Technical Validation

### 6.1 Independent governance and evidence review

An independent governance and evidence review may assess scope, approved source categories, provenance, unsupported claims, contradictions, uncertainty, abstention, claim-specific authority, Technology-First A–I compliance, and improper downstream implementation.

A separately initialized AI review session may perform this function only when it did not author or materially edit the package, did not select/control evidence, did not predetermine the result, and records its source basis, review scope, output, and limitations.

An AI governance review is not technical certification.

### 6.2 External technical validation

External technical validation is required when a technical claim cannot be established reliably from approved evidence categories. It may include manufacturer confirmation, authoritative datasheet evidence, applicable standards/regulator evidence, laboratory results, measurements, tests, validated simulations, or issue-specific technical assessment by a suitably competent specialist.

It must be issue-specific and evidence-specific and must not create candidate search, ranking, appointment packages, permanent reviewer roles, standing technical-expert roles, or CV/personal credential files in the repository.

The technical evidence or validation result, not the individual’s identity or appointment status, is the governed object.

Each external technical validation engagement requires a separate, bounded, issue-specific owner authorization identifying the exact claim, the required evidence category, and the permitted validation method. No external engagement is authorized by this decision. The engagement record governs the evidence obtained; no CV, credential file, candidate record, or personal identity record enters the repository.

## 7. Independence Standard

No reviewer or review session may independently review a package that it authored, materially edited, controlled, directed, selected evidence for, predetermined, or previously approved without independent challenge.

A review is not independent merely because it runs in a separate chat window, uses a different model name, restates the package, declares itself independent, or did not write the final wording but materially shaped the evidence or conclusion.

The review record must state prior authorship/editing context, source basis, exact scope, conflicts/limitations, conclusion, and required corrections.

If independence cannot be established: INDEPENDENCE FAILURE — RE-REVIEW REQUIRED.

## 8. Competence Without Candidate Evaluation or Appointment

Competence applies to the evidence category, validation method, technical method, authority supporting the claim, and scope of external validation. It must not be converted back into candidate profiles, scoring, CV collection, appointment evidence, or reviewer appointment records.

Where evidence is insufficient, the package must use one or more of: DATASHEET REQUIRED; AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE REQUIRED; MEASUREMENT REQUIRED; TEST REQUIRED; SIMULATION REQUIRED; EXTERNAL TECHNICAL EVIDENCE REQUIRED; EXTERNAL SPECIALIST VALIDATION REQUIRED; CONTRADICTION UNRESOLVED; CANNOT VERIFY / ABSTAIN.

The system must not invent values, thresholds, tolerances, standards, test outcomes, or specialist conclusions.

## 13A. Structured Technical Guidance Output Model

For every D13 or TKP output that addresses an unresolved technical matter, InventorAI must structure the guidance using the following fields:

1. Domain.
2. Subdomain.
3. Technology.
4. Exact unresolved technical subproblem.
5. Missing technical information.
6. Required evidence.
7. Required technology, technical topic, or research subdomain.
8. Suggested technical search terms.
9. Validation method.
10. Required measurements, documents, datasheets, standards, tests, simulations, software, or tools.
11. What InventorAI can verify.
12. What InventorAI cannot verify.
13. Risk, uncertainty, contradiction, or abstention state.
14. Relevant specialist category, only where external expertise remains necessary.

The required output priority is:

TECHNOLOGY OR TECHNICAL TOPIC  
→ MISSING INFORMATION OR EVIDENCE  
→ VALIDATION METHOD  
→ SPECIALIST CATEGORY ONLY WHERE NECESSARY

InventorAI must not default to a broad statement such as “consult an electrical engineer” when it can identify a more precise technical category, such as embedded sensor-interface, analog-interface, PCB signal-integrity, PCB power-integrity, drone propulsion, flight-control, solar-system design, energy-storage, or grid-integration expertise.

The schema is designed for future domain-pack expansion, including PCB, drone systems, renewable energy, energy storage, and grid-integration domains. This decision does not authorize those domains, their research, or their implementation. The current executable scope remains electronics/electrical-only until a separate owner decision authorizes a new domain package.
