## 2. WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW

### 2.1 About inventor development

We do not know whether the platform produces genuine inventor development.
The signal record (S-2, S-4 confirmed; S-1, S-3, S-5 absent) is
consistent with two competing explanations: (a) the platform reliably
elicits ownership articulation and cross-gap reasoning transfer, or
(b) the participant produces consistent responses to a consistent question
sequence and the signals reflect question structure as much as inventor
behavior. The current evidence cannot distinguish between these.
Source: INVENTOR_OUTCOME_MEASUREMENT.md §2.3, STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md §2.3.

We do not know whether S-1 is absent because the inventor does not
self-correct, or because the platform does not generate the questions
that create S-1 conditions. Both explanations are active. Idea A Session 1
did not generate the fail-state question for Candidate 5.
Source: STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md D-4, INVENTOR_OUTCOME_MEASUREMENT.md FN-1.

We do not know whether the specificity and causal reasoning improvements
visible within sessions represent change in kind or change in degree.
SA-001A's governing criterion for stage boundaries requires change in kind.
This criterion has not been tested.
Source: STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md §9.

### 2.2 About the platform's measurement capability

We do not know whether inventors who score high on S-1 through S-6
demonstrate better reasoning on independent measures. The signals are
face-valid and theoretically grounded, but have not been empirically
validated against external criteria.
Source: INVENTOR_OUTCOME_MEASUREMENT.md §4.3.

We do not know whether the cross-session signal pattern (S-2, S-4 stable;
S-1, S-3, S-5 absent) is specific to this participant, this domain, or
this question set. Single-participant evidence cannot generalize.
Source: INVENTOR_OUTCOME_MEASUREMENT.md L-4, STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md L-4.

We do not know whether the assess_response() quality gate can distinguish
genuine mechanism understanding from fluent non-specific responses at scale.
DVO-S1-003 demonstrated it cannot in at least one observed case.
Source: STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md D-3.

### 2.3 About stage readiness

We do not know whether Stage 3 produces the epistemic outcomes it is
designed to produce. Stage 3 exit criteria are not enforced at runtime.
Stage 3 completion in all sessions is question delivery, not
evaluation-gated exit.
Source: STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md §1.2, L-3.

We do not know whether an inventor who completes Stage 2 has demonstrated
sufficient understanding of their mechanism to benefit from Stage 3.
H-1 is consistent with but not confirmed by current evidence.
Source: STAGE_EVOLUTION_POSITION.md §4.

### 2.4 About platform identity

We do not know the final platform identity classification (Inventor
Development Platform, Hybrid System, or Idea Development Platform).
§8 Step 2 cannot return YES or NO until Idea A has at least two sessions
and the full signal record is stable.
Source: OWNER_CORRECTION_DECISION.md; ILT002_EXECUTION_GUIDE.md §8.

## 5. EVIDENCE GAPS REMAINING

These are the specific evidence items whose absence blocks authorized
claims from being made.

### Gap 1 -- Idea A Session 2 (blocking §8)

**What is missing:** A second Idea A session with full transcript,
signal classification, and comparison against Session 1.

**What it blocks:** §8 Step 2 application; platform identity
classification; ER-1.

**Nature of gap:** Campaign execution -- not infrastructure or
governance.

### Gap 2 -- Timing table lock for Idea A (blocking S-6)

**What is missing:** ILT002_EMERGENCE_TIMING_TABLE.md Part 1 populated
and locked with Idea A Session 1 emergence data.

**What it blocks:** S-6 evaluation; FORM T completion; any cross-idea
timing comparison.

**Nature of gap:** Administrative -- the data exists in the transcript;
the lock record has not been executed.

### Gap 3 -- S-1 from a fail-state question (blocking development evidence)

**What is missing:** At least one session where the platform generates
a question that surfaces the primary S-1 structural opportunity, and
the inventor's response is classified against the S-1 anchor.

**What it blocks:** E-2 (S-1 confirmed in at least one session);
H-4 resolution (is S-1 absent due to inventor behavior or question
coverage?); confidence level 4.1 remains VERY LOW.

**Nature of gap:** Question coverage -- the platform did not generate
the fail-state question in Idea A Session 1. Whether this reflects
question set design, gap cascade logic, or session state is not yet
determined.

### Gap 4 -- Stage 3 evaluator integration (blocking stage 3 completion claim)

**What is missing:** stage3_evaluator.py integrated into run_iteration();
at least one session completing Stage 3 with evaluator-confirmed exit.

**What it blocks:** E-1 (Stage 2-3 functional completion confirmed);
the claim that "Stage 3 is functionally complete"; C-1 blocking
condition for stage evolution.

**Nature of gap:** Implementation -- the evaluator exists and is tested.
Integration requires a code change and separate authorization.

### Gap 5 -- Multi-participant evidence (blocking generalization)

**What is missing:** At least one additional participant completing the
Stage 2-3 sequence with signal record and transcript.

**What it blocks:** E-5 (multi-participant evidence); generalization
of any finding beyond this participant; confidence that signal pattern
is not specific to one inventor.

**Nature of gap:** Campaign design -- ILT-002 is single-participant by
Decision D-1. Multi-participant evidence requires a separate campaign.

### Gap 6 -- MVP scope freeze formal revision (blocking authorized domain claim)

**What is missing:** Formal execution of MVP_SCOPE_FREEZE.md revision
protocol against ILT-002 evidence -- documenting which criteria are met
and committing the revision decision.

**What it blocks:** The acknowledged inconsistency between the freeze's
"electronics/electrical only" language and the four active domains in
the repository. Until formally revised, the freeze technically still
restricts scope.

**Nature of gap:** Governance administrative -- the evidence to support
revision may exist from ILT-001 and ILT-002 combined; it has not been
formally evaluated against the revision criteria.

### Gap 7 -- GD-002 resolution (blocking Engineering Assessment Layer)

**What is missing:** Owner decision resolving GD-002 (Technical Guidance
Without Decision Ownership) -- specifically whether and how the platform
may present alternatives and explain trade-offs within the constitutive
boundaries C-1 and C-2.

**What it blocks:** ER-2 (Layer 1 before Layer 2); SO-2 (Engineering
Assessment Layer) becoming more than a strategic option.

**Nature of gap:** Governance decision -- requires owner resolution.
Cannot be resolved by analysis or implementation.
