## 5. Preserved Prohibition

The following prohibitions are preserved in full. Nothing in this document
weakens, narrows, or creates an exception to any of them.

InventorAI does not certify, approve, guarantee, or declare final real-world
readiness, compliance, viability, or professional conclusion for matters
including:

- regulatory approval or compliance;
- certification requirements;
- EMC or electromagnetic compatibility;
- manufacturing readiness;
- supply-chain viability;
- commercial viability;
- build or production readiness;
- or other domain-specific execution requirements.

InventorAI is not:

- a feasibility oracle;
- an implementation-readiness or production-readiness certifier;
- a commercial-success oracle;
- a legal, patentability, prior-art-clearance, claim-validity or
  freedom-to-operate conclusion authority;
- or an unsupported physical-validation authority.

Completing an InventorAI journey does not itself constitute external
validation, physical validation, manufacturing validation, regulatory
validation, commercial validation, legal validation, or any equivalent
external professional certification.

The Coverage Declaration obligations of the original §4 are preserved
unchanged: domain packs must declare their covered areas, not-covered areas,
and known limitations explicitly, and no domain pack may imply coverage it
does not provide.

## 6. Amended Governing Effect — Coverage Declaration Principle

### 6.1 Prospective clarification of "assess"

    PROSPECTIVE GOVERNING CLARIFICATION: YES
    RETROACTIVE REINTERPRETATION: NO

From this amendment's EFFECTIVE authoritative adoption, the governing effect of
the relevant original §4 language is prospectively bounded as set out in this
section. This document makes no claim about what §4 did or did not prohibit
before that adoption. The historical §4 wording, and its authority at the time,
remain preserved as truthful historical evidence.

Prospectively, the prohibition carried by the original use of the word

    assess

applies to assessment represented as certification, approval, guarantee, or
final real-world readiness or viability determination.

It does not prohibit separately authorized, evidence-grounded product
capabilities from assessing governed inputs and admitted evidence within
applicable evidence, provenance and validation boundaries.

### 6.2 Governed inputs and admitted evidence

Decision-support reasoning may use governed inputs together with admitted
evidence. Admissible evidence may include, where separately authorized and
properly admitted through governed provenance and validation boundaries,
user-provided evidence, external test evidence, manufacturer feedback,
simulation artifacts, market evidence, or other evidence so admitted.

The governing rule is that the platform may derive bounded decision-support
conclusions from governed inputs and admitted evidence while preserving
provenance, validation status, uncertainty, source authority, and epistemic
status.

This is a Level 0 semantic permission boundary only. It authorizes no external
provider, no external-data ingestion, no human study, no manufacturer
integration, no simulation system, no market-research execution, no evidence
acquisition workflow, and no implementation. The mechanisms by which any
evidence becomes admitted remain governed elsewhere and require their own
separate authorization.

### 6.3 Permitted support

Where separately authorized and evidence-supported, InventorAI may:

- assess governed inputs and admitted evidence relevant to technical
  readiness, prototype readiness, manufacturing readiness, integration
  readiness, whole-system readiness, or commercial readiness;
- organize and synthesize governed evidence;
- identify missing evidence, unresolved dependencies, assumptions, risks,
  conflicts, and Known Unknowns;
- derive bounded decision support;
- prepare structured handoffs;
- and route the user toward the next justified evidence-gathering,
  validation, prototype, manufacturing-preparation, integration-validation,
  or commercial-validation action.

Listing an activity here is a permission boundary only. It is not a statement
that the activity exists, is implemented, is scheduled, or is authorized. Each
listed activity remains subject to its applicable existing authoritative owner
or owners, where established, or to a later Owner-adjudicated ownership
disposition, together with any separately required implementation authority.
Nothing in this amendment creates or selects such an owner or contract.

### 6.4 Binding non-equivalence

    ASSIST
    / ASSESS GOVERNED INPUTS AND ADMITTED EVIDENCE
    / ORGANIZE
    / IDENTIFY GAPS
    / PREPARE HANDOFF
    / ROUTE TO NEXT ACTION

    !=

    CERTIFY
    / APPROVE
    / GUARANTEE
    / DECLARE FINAL REAL-WORLD READINESS
    / DECLARE COMMERCIAL SUCCESS
    / DECLARE PRODUCTION READINESS
    / DECLARE LEGAL OR PATENT CONCLUSIONS

Support of the first kind must never be represented, presented, exported,
summarized, or relied upon as an outcome of the second kind.

### 6.5 Bounded negative and uncertain outcomes

Truthful outcomes including `UNKNOWN`, `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`, `HOLD` and
`REQUIRES VALIDATION` are preserved as valid and product-valuable, wherever the
governing architecture supports them.

A negative outcome must be bounded to a specified next step and to the current
governed evidence — for example, that an idea is not ready for a specified next
step under the currently governed evidence, or materially equivalent wording.

No unqualified universal `NOT READY` is introduced by this amendment, and none
may be issued as though InventorAI were making a permanent or final real-world
conclusion. The platform may truthfully conclude that currently governed
evidence is insufficient for a specified next action. It must not transform
such a bounded conclusion into a universal declaration that an invention is
permanently or finally not manufacturable, not commercially viable, not
buildable, or otherwise conclusively invalid.

Absence of decisive evidence must not become a positive readiness claim. AI
inference must not be represented as physical evidence. Unknowns must not be
silently closed. No fabricated business-success percentage, readiness
percentage, or other false precision may be produced. No false certainty.

## 12. Final Owner Ruling

As a Level 0 owner amendment, this document records and gives governing effect,
upon and only upon satisfaction of §9 of this document, to the owner's
decision that:

1. InventorAI remains prohibited from acting as a certifier, guarantor,
   feasibility oracle, production-readiness certifier, commercial-success
   oracle, legal or IP conclusion authority, or unsupported physical-validation
   authority.

2. InventorAI is not thereby prohibited from legitimately assisting, assessing
   governed inputs and admitted evidence, organizing evidence, identifying
   gaps, preparing handoffs, and routing next actions, where separately
   authorized and evidence-supported.

   as an outcome of the first kind.

4. Bounded, truthful negative and uncertain outcomes remain valid and
   product-valuable, and must not be inflated into permanent or final
   real-world conclusions.

5. The current Level-0 product identity is unchanged, and
   `docs/governance/OWNER_PRODUCT_IDENTITY_CORRECTION.md` is preserved with its
   governing scope unmodified.

6. No implementation, capability activation, evidence-acquisition mechanism,
   owner, engine, schema, or scoring system is created or authorized by this
   amendment.
