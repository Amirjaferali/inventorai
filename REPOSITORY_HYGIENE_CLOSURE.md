## Why Exclusions Were Correct

**No implicit authority granted.**  
Untracked artifacts carry zero operational authority regardless of content quality or apparent completeness.

**No incomplete CLASS-3 evidence promoted.**  
Both named baselines contained partial provenance metadata — including `prompt_sha256`, `model`,
timestamps, and benchmark identity — but did not contain sufficient provenance for CLASS-3
designation. Specifically, explicit `schema_version` and `engine_version` fields were absent
from the retained metadata. Partial provenance is not sufficient provenance. The governance
freeze was designed precisely to prevent "almost sufficient" evidence relaxation.

**No self-approved generated governance artifacts committed.**  
The generator script was not only producing governance documents, but also generating the
approval registry that asserted their approval state. This created a recursive self-authorizing
governance surface. A script cannot approve its own outputs. Presence of approval markers
inside the generator is circularity, not authority.

---

## Circularity Discovery Sequence

The Approval Circularity failure was discovered through:

1. Provenance challenge during Commit 2 scope expansion — the `docs/` directory contained
   significantly more artifacts than the original hygiene scope revealed
2. Generator linkage inspection — `grep` against `scripts/write_governance_docs.py` confirmed
   the suspect docs were script outputs, not hand-authored artifacts
3. Detection of embedded approval assertions inside the generator itself — the script contained
   an approval registry asserting `APPROVED` status for its own outputs
4. Application of the Approval Circularity Test from frozen Document A policy — approval
   evidence must be external, immutable, and independently authorable; a generator asserting
   its own outputs are approved fails this test unconditionally

This sequence prevented eight generated governance artifacts from receiving implicit CLASS-1
authority through convenience classification.
